    Selection Process | Experts Program

    The Experts Program selection process is currently underway and will continue through November 15, 2026. However, some applicants may receive their acceptance notifications before this date.

    During this period, please keep the following information in mind:

    Interview Invitations: You may receive an email or message inviting you to schedule an interview with an Expert. Please check your email and messages regularly.

    Interview Participation: Being invited to an interview does not guarantee acceptance into the program.

    Multiple Interviews: Applicants may be invited to more than one interview, depending on the Experts' selection process.

    Interviews Are Not Mandatory: Not all applicants will be invited to an interview. Some may be selected without an interview.

    Selection Results: Acceptance notifications may be sent throughout the selection process. Final results will be announced on November 15.

    Haven't heard back yet? Don't worry! This does not mean your application has been rejected. Your application may still be under review until the final results are announced.

    If you have any questions, please contact us at expertsprogram@metaanalysis.academy.
